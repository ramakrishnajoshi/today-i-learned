"Belt-and-suspenders" means using extra safety steps or two methods to prevent failure. [1] 
## Meaning and Usage

* Extra caution: It describes a choice driven by fear of failure. [1] 
* Finance and law: People use the [Investopedia Financial Definition](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/b/belt-and-suspenders.asp) to describe very strict lending rules or safety checks. [2] 
* General definition: Read the [Merriam-Webster Definition](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/belt-and-suspenders) for more details on using double procedures. [1] 

## Fashion Rule

* Pick one: You should not wear a belt and suspenders at the same time. [3] 
* Same job: Both items hold up pants, so wearing both creates a cluttered look. [3]
