Steelmanning is the argumentative tactic of creating the strongest, most logical version of an opposing viewpoint—even better than how the opponent stated it—before attacking or evaluating it. It is the exact opposite of a straw man argument, which distorts or weakens an opponent's view to make it easy to knock down. [1, 2, 3] 
## Core Concepts of Steelmanning

* Definition: Improving and building up an adversary’s position to test an idea in its absolute best light. 
* Goal: Pursuing truth, finding common ground, and stress-testing logic rather than just winning a debate. 
* Application: Commonly used in philosophy, policy-making, complex negotiations, and everyday critical thinking. 

## Alternative Meanings

* Steel Man of India: A historical nickname famously given to Dr. Jamshed J Irani for his massive contributions to the Indian steel industry with Tata Steel.
* Companies & Trade: A descriptor or name used by various industrial entities, such as Steelman-KLM Forge (engineering forgings) and Steelman Telecom (network services).
* Construction Trade: A worker who handles and ties steel reinforcement bars (rebar) on a building site.
