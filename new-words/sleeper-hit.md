A sleeper hit is a term used in the entertainment industry for a movie, TV show, album, or game that becomes a massive financial success despite having little to no promotion or low initial expectations.
Unlike big-budget blockbusters that rely on massive marketing campaigns to break records on their very first weekend, a sleeper hit starts slow and grows steadily over time.
Ex: Premada Oorali movie : Released quietly but sustained incredible theatrical occupancy for weeks, eventually racing toward the ₹100 crore club.
