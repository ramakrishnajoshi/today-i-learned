# Things I Don't Want to Forget

A collection of ideas, sentences and principles worth revisiting.

---

## Life

> **Don't confuse "not urgent" with "not important."**

The things that matter most later are often the things that feel easiest to postpone today.

Start early. Be consistent. Take care of your health, money and relationships before they become urgent.

---

## Career / Leadership

> **Your manager's cognitive load should decrease because you're involved, not increase.**

A senior person shouldn't merely complete difficult tasks. Their involvement should reduce uncertainty, decision-making burden and stress for the people above and around them.

---

## Career

> **Don't just bring problems upward. Bring analysis, options and recommendations.**

---

## Money

> **You can catch up on money later, but you can't buy back decades of compounding.**

---

## Relationships

> **Don't postpone family time until life becomes less busy.**
